# Pair Kiss Stage-1 Couple Scripts

Stage 1 = "Together" (before the kiss). Speaker order A, B, A, B. 30 unique ordered-pair scripts, 120 lines. The 5 prototype scripts from Kiss_Lines_1.0 (BW-RG, EF-BC, IW-LS, BC-LS, RG-IW) are kept verbatim.

ID format: `S1-AA-BB-n` (AA = speaker A, BB = speaker B, n = 1-4; A says 1 and 3, B says 2 and 4).
